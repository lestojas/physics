Falling Target

The classic demo: a projectile is aimed directly at a target and fired at the exact instant the target is released to fall. Does it hit, or miss below? Aim, fire, and watch.

Turn the speed way down to see the projectile fall short — but notice it still lands exactly where the target is, just lower.

projectile target line of sight equal drop ½gt²
Answer: the projectile always hits the falling target. The moment gravity switches on, both objects begin dropping the same vertical distance (½gt²) in the same time — regardless of the projectile's horizontal motion. So the projectile falls below the straight aim line by exactly the amount the target falls below its start. The horizontal and vertical motions simply don't affect each other.
