PHYlip's Simulators

A small collection of interactive demos that reveal how horizontal and vertical motion work independently in projectile physics. Pick a simulator from the sidebar, or a card below.

Simulator 1

Falling Target

Aim a projectile directly at a target and fire the instant it's released. It always hits — because both fall the same distance in the same time.

 Simulator 2

Velocity Components

See how a launch velocity at an angle splits into an independent horizontal push and vertical push, and how the trade-off changes with angle.
The common thread: in every one of these demos, horizontal and vertical motion don't affect each other. Gravity only touches the vertical part, while the horizontal part just cruises along at constant speed.
