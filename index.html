PHYlip Simulators

A collection of interactive physics simulators

Falling Target

Aim a projectile directly at a target and fire the instant it's released. It always hits — because both fall the same distance in the same time.

Projectile Motion

Explore the three common cases of projectile motion — launched horizontally, at an angle onto level ground, or at an angle onto ground of a different height.
