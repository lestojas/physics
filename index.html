PHYlip Simulators

A collection of interactive physics simulators

Tangential Speed

Uniform circular motion: v = 2πr / T. Change the radius and period and watch how fast the object moves along its circular path.

Period & Frequency

f = 1 / T. Period is the time for one revolution; frequency is revolutions per second, shown with a live counter.

Centripetal Acceleration

a_c = v² / r. See the acceleration vector point to the center and grow with speed squared as you change v and r.

Centripetal Force

F_c = m v² / r. The net force that holds an object in its circle, growing with mass and speed squared.

Falling Target

Aim a projectile directly at a target and fire the instant it's released. It always hits — because both fall the same distance in the same time.

Projectile Motion

Explore the three common cases of projectile motion — launched horizontally, at an angle onto level ground, or at an angle onto ground of a different height.
