PHYlip Simulators

A collection of interactive physics simulators

Game Show

🏆 Physics League: Kinematics Championship

The live host screen for the gamified league. Built-in per-question timer, manual answer reveal, click the groups that got it right, and automatic running totals for all 6 teams — with every twist (Double Trouble, Flip First, Mystery Multiplier, Wildcard) built in.

Uniform Circular Motion

A 4-in-1 simulator. Pick what to explore — tangential speed (v = 2πr / T), period & frequency (f = 1 / T), centripetal acceleration (a_c = v² / r), or centripetal force (F_c = m v² / r) — and watch the vectors update.

Falling Target

Aim a projectile directly at a target and fire the instant it's released. It always hits — because both fall the same distance in the same time.

Projectile Motion

Explore the three common cases of projectile motion — launched horizontally, at an angle onto level ground, or at an angle onto ground of a different height.
