Deriving Kinematic Equation 3
Eliminating time to relate velocity, acceleration, and displacement
Goal v² = v₀² + 2a(x − x₀)
Equations you will combine
Equation 1:  v = v₀ + at
Equation 4:  x − x₀ = ½(v₀ + v)·t
1 Your goal is to eliminate t. Start with Equation 1: v = v₀ + at. Subtract v₀ from both sides, then divide both sides by a to isolate t. Show each algebraic step:
Step-by-step: v − v₀ = ___ → t = ___
2 Now take the displacement equation (Equation 4 rearranged): x − x₀ = ½(v₀ + v)·t. Replace t with your expression from Step 1. Write the full substitution:
x − x₀ = ½(v₀ + v) · [    ]
3 Multiply the two brackets in the numerator. Notice that (v₀ + v)(v − v₀) is a difference of squares pattern: (A + B)(A − B) = A² − B² where A = v and B = v₀. Apply this identity and simplify:
(v + v₀)(v − v₀) = v² − v₀² → x − x₀ = (v² − v₀²) / ___
4 You should now have: x − x₀ = (v² − v₀²) / 2a. Multiply both sides by 2a to clear the denominator, then add v₀² to both sides to isolate v². Write the final equation:
First: 2a(x − x₀) = ___ → Then: v² = ___
